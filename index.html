How to Use

  • Upload: Select one or multiple images from your device.
  • Trim: Set a percentage to cut off the bottom (great for removing watermarks). The red area in the preview shows what will be gone.
  • Add Sizes: Enter width and height, choose your unit (px, cm, inch), and click Add.
  • Process: Click the button to crop all images to all sizes instantly.
  • Download: A ZIP file containing all your cropped variations will download automatically.

*Note: Non-pixel units (cm, in) are converted assuming 96 DPI screen resolution.

What does the Bulk Image Cropper do?

This tool crops and resizes a whole batch of images to multiple target sizes in one pass, entirely in your browser. It's built for anyone who needs the same creative in several dimensions at once — an ad campaign running 1250×600 and 1200×630 versions of the same banner, or a product shot that needs a square and a landscape crop for different placements.

Because everything runs client-side, your images are never uploaded to a server. Files stay on your device the whole time; the cropping happens locally using the browser's canvas engine, and the output is zipped and downloaded directly.

To use it: upload your source images, add as many target sizes as you need (in px, in, cm, or mm), and optionally set a bottom-trim percentage to strip out a watermark or footer strip before cropping. Every crop is centred automatically, and the preview shows exactly what will be kept before you commit.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Bulk Image Cropper free to use?

Yes. It's completely free, with no account, watermark, or usage limit. You can crop as many images as you like.

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Every crop is generated locally in your browser using the Canvas API — your files never leave your device or touch a server.

What image formats are supported?

You can upload any common image format your browser can decode (JPG, PNG, WebP, and more). Output is exported as JPG for consistent file sizes across all your crops.

Can I crop to more than one size at the same time?

Yes — add as many target sizes as you need, and each uploaded image will be cropped to every size and bundled into a single ZIP download.

What's the "Bottom Trim" option for?

It removes a percentage of the image from the bottom before cropping — commonly used to strip out a watermark, caption bar, or footer strip that shouldn't appear in the final crop.
